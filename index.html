Chapter 10: The Invisible Man

Author: Eric Mandaluyong Ericson

Once upon a time in a far eastern science program, there lived a scientist named Dilemma who desired to become an invisible man. While his colleague Blakemort sought only to avoid destruction and preserve order, Dilemma wanted invisibility to scare people.

Dilemma began the dangerous procedure by stabbing his left arm with a localized invisibility serum. As the chemical spread rapidly through his circulatory system, he became completely invisible to the naked human eye. Driven by chaos, Dilemma began terrorizing citizens.

When Blakemort discovered Dilemma's horrifying actions, he immediately attempted to intervene: "Please stop it Dilemma! If you scare people while invisible, 50% survive while 50% commit suicide or die from extreme terror!"

Refusing to listen, Dilemma embraced his role as a murderer. To prevent further catastrophe, Blakemort was forced to neutralize Dilemma due to the overexposure and untamed lethality of the serum.

Moral Lesson

"Don't pretend to be invisible most of the times. 50% of them get scared and die because of too much exposure and without following instruction/direction, so its cost is death."
